About The Quiz

How Replaceable Are You?

How Replaceable Are You? is a 60-second quiz that asks five questions about your work and uses AI to estimate how much of it could be automated in the coming years.

How it works

You type short answers in your own words. Your answers are sent to an AI model, which returns a score from 1 to 99 (higher means easier to automate), a short label, a two-sentence explanation and three tips for staying valuable. If the AI is busy, a simple built-in estimate is used instead.

What it isn't

It's entertainment, not career advice. No quiz can predict what will happen to a real job, and every job is a mix of tasks. Use your score as a conversation starter, not a verdict.

Your privacy

We don't ask for your name and we don't create accounts. Please don't type personal or sensitive information. See the privacy policy.

Why we built it

Everyone is talking about AI and jobs, but most of the conversation is either panic or hype. We wanted something quick, honest and shareable that gets people thinking about which human skills matter most.
