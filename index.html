    About

    How Replaceable Are You? is a 60-second quiz that asks five questions about your work and uses AI to estimate how much of it could be automated in the coming years.

    How it works: you type short answers in your own words. They're sent to an AI model, which returns a score from 1 to 99 (higher means easier to automate), a short label, a two-sentence explanation and three tips. If the AI is busy, a simple built-in estimate is used instead.

    What it isn't: entertainment, not career advice. No quiz can predict what will happen to a real job.

    Your privacy: we don't ask for your name or create accounts. Please don't type personal or sensitive information.
